# Roadmap

- **First public release** — Prepare the extension for Chrome and Edge.
- **Web search and research** — Look up information to clarify what you’re reading or research a topic, with citations.
- **Product comparisons** — Find similar tools and compare them using cited sources.
- **Documentation and repository indexing** — Search across more of a docs site or codebase and link to the relevant pages and files.
- **Papers and PDFs** — Read and explain research papers and text-based PDFs.
